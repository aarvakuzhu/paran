# Paran – Inventory (PWA)

Know what you have, where it is, and who has it, for your home and the groups you run.
Live at **https://aarvakuzhu.github.io/paran/**. This repo continues the Stash pilot (`aarvakuzhu/stash`, frozen; backup branch `backup/stash-final-2026-10-16`). Inventories live in each user's Google Drive, so the same inventories open here.

**Sharing the site with the Stash pilot:** both apps are on `aarvakuzhu.github.io`, so they share the phone's storage. Paran keeps its own `paran.*` keys, its own `paran` IndexedDB and `paran-*` caches; on first run it copies the pilot's `stash.*` keys and pending photos once, and never changes them. "Get the latest version" only touches Paran's own worker and caches.


Know what you have, where it is, and what it's for. A static web app (no server, no database) that keeps everything in **the user's own Google Drive**:

```
My Drive/
└── Stash – Home Inventory/
    ├── Stash Inventory        ← Google Sheet: Items, Locations, Members, Log tabs
    └── Photos/                ← full-size photos (≤1600px JPEG)
```

The app only asks for the `drive.file` scope, so it can see **only the files it created** (or the one sheet someone picks to join). It cannot read anything else in the user's Drive.

## Screens

- **Changing access:** in More → People & access (or a group page), the owner taps a person to switch **Can edit / Can view**, **Remove** them, or **Make them the owner** (Google emails them to accept; until then the old owner stays owner; photos the old owner added stay in their Drive). Invites also turn off "editors can share", so only the owner can add people.
- **More** is a short menu: the current inventory (switch, rename, add), People & access, AI suggestions, Your data, About, and Sign out. Each opens its own page.
- **Roles: Owner, Can edit, Can view.** Invites (More → People for one inventory, or the group page for a whole group) have a **Can edit / Can view** choice. *Can view* shares the sheet (or group folder) as Google Drive *reader*, so Google itself refuses changes. A viewer's app shows **View only** in the status pill, hides Add, Edit, Move, Lend, Scan many, photo changes, restore and other edit actions, and keeps search, browsing, Share and QR scanning. Access is re-checked with Drive each time the app opens; if a change is refused mid-session because access was reduced, the app re-checks and switches to view-only. Viewers don't create a photo folder or update sheet headers.
- **Several inventories, and groups of them:** tap the inventory name at the top (or More → Inventory) for **Your inventories**: switch, **New inventory**, **New group** (an organization, or your homes) and **Open one shared with you**. Each inventory is its own Google Sheet and folder, with its own places, people and history; the app reopens straight into the one you pick, from this phone's copy. More → Inventory also has **Rename this inventory** (renames the sheet and folder).
  - **In Drive, everything sits under one "Paran" folder** in the creator's My Drive (never shared itself): groups inside it, inventories inside their group (or straight inside Paran when not in one), and each inventory's Photos inside its folder. Photos someone adds to an inventory they don't own go in *their* Paran folder as "Photos – <inventory>". The Paran folder is only made when there's something to put in it, so opening a shared group as a viewer adds nothing to your Drive. Groups and inventories made before this folder existed are moved into it the first time the owner opens the new version (only ones at the top of My Drive; anything they filed elsewhere stays put). People shared a group or inventory still find it under *Shared with me*.
```
My Drive / Paran /
  Tamil School /                 group (share this = group access)
    Tamil School – directory     Inventories, People, Events, EventLines
    School /                     inventory (share its sheet = inventory access)
      School                     Items, Locations, Members, Log, Tasks
      Photos /
  Home /                         an inventory not in a group
```
  - **A group** is one Drive folder holding its inventories' folders plus a **directory** sheet (Inventories and People tabs). Inviting someone on the **group page** shares the folder, so they can open and edit every inventory in it, including ones added later. Inviting from More → People still shares a single inventory.
  - **Joining a group:** the invited person chooses *Open one shared with you* and picks "<Group> – directory". Google only lets the app open files a person picks, so the app then asks them to tick the group's inventories once (the picker opens inside the group's folder). New inventories added later show "Tap to open it on this phone".
  - **Search covers every inventory.** Results from the one you're in come first, then "In your other inventories", each under its inventory (and group) name. Inventories with a copy on this phone are searched instantly; the rest are fetched in the background when online (and refreshed if the copy is over 10 minutes old), then kept on the phone. Offline, a banner at the top says so and names any inventory that couldn't be checked. Tapping a result opens that inventory at the item. Chips under the search box (one per inventory with matches, with its count) drop an inventory from the results; tap again to bring it back. They reset when the search is cleared. Group inventories not yet opened on this phone show "Include …" (one tap via the picker).
  - New inventories can be created inside a group by its owner; an existing inventory you own can be added to a group (its folder moves into the group's folder). Photo folders that members create are shared with the inventory's people and the group's people.
- **Help** (**?** at the top of every screen): opens on topics for the screen you're on, then searches 54 "How do I…?" topics in your own words (offline, no AI; other phrasings and near spellings match). Each answer has steps in the words on the buttons, who it's for, related topics and **Take me there** (opens the screen and pulses the button). Questions about your things ("where is my drill") offer to search your items instead. *Did this help?* and *Tell us what you were trying to do* are kept on the phone, with questions Help couldn't answer; More → Your activity lists them with **Share with the Paran team** (your phone's share sheet). **Ask AI** (with AI suggestions on): tap *Ask AI* or press Enter. The phone picks the 8 most relevant topics (search weighted by how rare each word is, topped up with this screen's and common ones) and sends only those, the question and a one-line description of the person (demo or Google, owner / can edit / can view, in a group or one inventory only, AI on, which screen) to Flash-Lite; no inventory contents. It answers only from those topics in the words on the buttons, shows the topics it used (topic ids not among those sent are dropped) with **Take me there**, says *Not in Paran's help yet* when they don't cover it, and allows one follow-up (which carries the first question and answer). Questions about your things are never sent; they go to item search. Did it help, a note on No, and unanswered questions are kept as help feedback; Your activity counts asks, unanswered, yes/no. **Why? beside messages:** a message that stops you or fails (view only, Google refused a change, sign in again, couldn't reach Google, AI key or allowance problems, AI couldn't read something, an invite that failed) shows a small **?**; nothing opens by itself. Tapping it opens Help with the message quoted and the topic that explains it, open. Such a message stays a little longer (6 s), and Help remembers the last one for 5 minutes: the **?** at the top then starts with *The message you just saw*. The same ? sits beside the AI key error and the Events notes for people whose events are run by a group. **Share these steps** (every topic): sends the question and its numbered steps with a link (`…/#/help/<topic>`) that opens exactly those steps in Paran, with Take me there. Opened before signing in, the steps show on the welcome screen (once) with a note, and open again once Paran is open; the link is then forgotten (or after 7 days). After inviting someone, the message offers **Send how to join** (the join steps, with the link). **Pictures:** most topics show one to three pictures of the real screens with the button to tap ringed in orange (swipe when there are several; tap one to enlarge). They're made from the app itself by `tools/help-shots.py` (demo sample data, or a stand-in Google group for signed-in screens; never anyone's data) from each topic's `shots` (screen, taps, what to ring, caption), and re-made by GitHub after every push that changes the app (`.github/workflows/help-shots.yml`, which commits what changed and asks Pages to rebuild). They load when a topic is opened, not with the app (about 15 KB each); the service worker keeps the ones seen, and one that can't load offline says *The picture shows when you're online*. The catalog is `js/help.js`; `tools/help-check.mjs` fails when an action in the code (`data-a`/`data-ev`/`data-m`/`data-act`) has no topic, a topic points at a button or screen that's gone, or a screen has no topic. It runs on every push (`.github/workflows/checks.yml`); `CLAUDE.md` asks every change to update the help with it.

The outline opens for editing first: rename, remove (×) or add places and what's inside them; groups also name the group and can add or remove inventories. **Create** makes it all (for a group: the group, every inventory in it and their places) and opens Places. *Start empty instead* names one inventory and goes to the quick place setup as before.
- **Find** (landing page, cards): greeting and search; category chips appear while searching. Then three count cards (**Items** opens the full list, **Out** turns amber when something is late, **Places**), **Needs attention** cards with a one-tap action (*Got it back* / *Picked up* / *Found it*), **Places** as photo tiles (a collage of what's inside), **Out now** person cards and **Recently added** photo cards (both scroll sideways). Items without a photo get a soft coloured tile. Typing replaces the dashboard with results; Select is on the results and All items lists.
- **Places**: your places and what's in them. Opening a place shows items kept **directly** in it, then each place inside it as a row with its item count (and how many places are inside it). Tap a row to **expand or collapse** its items in place; tap the **›** at the right to open that place. **Expand all / Collapse all** toggles every row; the open/closed state is remembered per place on the device. **Add item here** and **Scan many** are the main actions; Bring items here, Share, Move, Rename, QR label and Remove place are in the ⋯ menu (containers also show a **QR label** button).
- **+**: add an item. Photo, name, **how many** (− / + stepper), what it’s for, where; category, extra search words, dates and notes are under *More details*. Adding a "not in inventory yet" event line starts with the quantity the event needs. The tab bar is hidden while you fill it in.
- **Out**: everything lent or reserved, grouped by person, overdue pick-ups first. A red badge on the tab counts late pick-ups.
- **More**: account, people the inventory is shared with, AI suggestions, export.
- **Your activity** (More → Your activity): usage counts kept on the phone only (`paran.stats`, 120 days) and shown to the person: days they used Paran with a day-by-day chart (7 days, 30 days or everything kept), how many items they added, moved, lent, got back, searched, events planned, Day-of ticks and After actions, AI uses with how right the names were, and their groups now (people, view-only, inventories, items). An off switch deletes them; "See exactly what's kept" shows the raw record. Kept: a random install ID; per day the version, demo/Google, installed or browser, inventories, in a group, AI on, the counts above, AI uses with weighted scores, and per group a snapshot under a hashed group ID that's the same on every member's phone. Never names, emails, items, places, photos or search words; the demo's sample data isn't counted. **Nothing is sent and there is no sending code**; how (or whether) counts are shared is still to be decided.
- **Your data** (More): owners get links to the sheets behind what they own, each saying what's in it: the inventory's sheet (items, places, people, history, tasks), the group's directory sheet if they own the group (events, inventories, people), and their Paran folder in Drive. A note says to make changes in the app. Everyone can download the open inventory as CSV and refresh; people who don't own the inventory are told whose Drive it's in. **Start fresh on this phone** (signed in only) forgets the phone's copy of inventories, groups, photos and the remembered Paran folder, and signs out; it keeps the AI key, Your activity and help feedback, and changes nothing in Drive. If the Paran folder is deleted in Drive, the app notices (its remembered folder is gone or in the trash) and makes a new one when needed.
- **Item page**: where it is, then **Move** and **Lend or reserve** (or *Got it back* / *Picked up* / *Found it*). Edit is the pencil; *Mark as missing* and *Remove* are in ⋯. **Back** returns to wherever you came from.
- **Events** (More → Events; the next two also show on Find under *Coming up*): an event has a name, an optional date and notes, and a list of what it needs in **sections** (Stage, Decor, AV, Food…). Each line points at an item, with how many are needed and a note; things nobody has logged yet can go on the list too ("Not in inventory yet") and get linked later with *Add to the inventory* or *It's already in an inventory*. The event page shows **readiness**: "12 of 18 ready", a coloured bar, and filter chips for **Ready** (in its place), **Short** (need more than there are), **With someone** (lent or reserved), **Missing**, **Not in inventory** and **Not checked**. **By section** lists the plan; **Where to get it** groups the same lines by place (and inventory) and by person, for whoever is collecting. Share sends the list as text. An item's page shows **Needed for** with the upcoming events that use it. Removing a line or deleting an event has Undo and never changes any inventory.
  - **A line can draw on several items**, in one inventory or across the group's: "Tablecloths ×40" counts the 15 in the school closet and the 25 in the storage unit. When you add something, items with the same name elsewhere are found and added to the line automatically (the toast says how many). Each is listed under the line with its place and count; tap **Not for this event** on any to leave it out of the total (**Use it** brings it back). The line sheet also shows **Also found** (other same-named items, one tap to add) and *Add another item to this line*. A new line drawing on several items starts with "how many needed" = their total; a single item starts at 1. *Where to get it* lists the line under each of its places.
  - **Adding to an event:** every tap adds straight away. Tick an item and it's on the list (tick again to take it back); type something nobody has logged and tap *Add “…”* or press Enter, and it's on the list with the keyboard still up for the next one. Closing the sheet never loses anything; the button at the bottom is just **Done · N added**. Sheets stay above the on-screen keyboard.
  - **Who's bringing it:** in a line's sheet, set **How many needed** (− / +, applies at once), then add people, each with their own number (− / + on their row, editable any time). A new person starts with whatever is still unassigned; the line shows "18 of 20 assigned · 2 left". Quick-picks list **people with access** to the inventory or group first (✓, linked to their Google account; "Me" for you), then names used before and people you lend to; a typed name or email that belongs to an account is linked automatically, and any row can be linked to an account from its picker. Works for things to buy and for things to pick up from where they're kept ("from Storage unit › Shelf 1"). *Still needed* shows who's on each buy-or-borrow item and flags **Nobody yet**. **Send each person their list** (Share, or Email for linked people); **Print everyone's lists** (a page per person, then what nobody has). Linked people see **You're bringing** on the home screen (per upcoming event) and on the event page. Stored as `bringers` on the line: name, qty, email.
  - **Still needed** (bottom of the event page): everything not ready yet, grouped by what someone has to do: **To buy or borrow** (not enough anywhere: "Tablecloths ×4 more, have 20 of 24", or not in any inventory), **To get back** (lent or reserved, and to whom), **To find** (marked missing, where it was last), **To check** (an inventory not on this phone). **Share** sends it as a message (WhatsApp, Messages, email) through the phone's share sheet; **Print** prints a clean page with tick boxes. The share button at the top also offers *Share / Print the full list*. When everything is ready the card says so.
  - **Linking by hand:** a line that isn't linked to anything opens with a search box (*Already logged? Find it*), filled with the line's name; if no item has all the words, close matches for any one word are shown. Tap *Link*, and same-named items elsewhere are added too. Linked lines have *Search and add another item to this line*.
  - **Plan / Day of / After:** each event has three tabs, and opens on the one that fits its date (before: Plan, on the day: Day of, after: After).
    - **Plan** is the list, readiness, Still needed and who's bringing what. In the week before, a card offers **Remind** (each person's list as a reminder, by share or email). The ⋯ menu has **Reserve its items**: reserves them for the event in whichever inventory they're in (so nobody lends them out; reserved-for-this-event counts as ready).
    - **Day of:** tick things as they arrive (big round tick), or tap the count for partial amounts ("8 of 10") and who brought them. **By person** shows each person's part with **All here**. "3 of 6 here" at the top; **Share what's not here yet**.
    - **After:** for each line, **Back in place** (frees a reservation for the event), **Somewhere else** (moves the item in the inventory), **Missing** (marks the item missing), **Damaged** (adds a dated note to the item); things that weren't in the inventory get **Keep it: add to inventory** (opens Add, then links the line) or **Used up or returned**. "6 of 6 sorted", **Share where things went**, and **Copy for next time** (same sections, items, quantities and notes, optionally who brings what; nothing ticked). These work on items in any of the group's inventories on this phone: the change goes to that inventory's sheet (only the changed cells, so photo previews stay) with a History entry there. Reserve also covers every inventory the event uses; an inventory not on this phone yet is named so it can be included first.
  - **In a group, events belong to the group.** They're kept in the group's directory sheet, so everyone in the group sees the same events from whichever of its inventories they have open, and a line can point at an item in **any** of the group's inventories (the school, the storage unit, a volunteer's garage). Other inventories are read from this phone's copy and refreshed in the background (at most every 10 minutes each), like search across inventories. An inventory the group has that isn't on this phone yet shows its lines as **Not checked**, with *Include …* (one tap through the picker). People who can only view the group see its events but can't change them. **Events always belong to a group.** Tapping *New event* in an inventory that isn't in one explains that Paran will put it in a group first (a new group, or one you already own), moves its folder there, offers to give its people the whole group, then opens the new event. Only the inventory's owner can do this; anyone else is told to ask the owner. (Events made in an inventory's own sheet before this, and in demo mode, stay there.)
  - **Who sees an event.** Access is given at two levels: the **group** (every inventory in it, and its events) or **one inventory**. On an inventory's People page in a group, people are listed as *Everyone in <group>* and *Only <inventory>*, with **Give whole group** on the second list; the invite form asks which of the two. Events are for group-level people only. Someone with only one inventory can still be given lines to bring: those reach them as **tasks** in that inventory's **Tasks** tab and show under *You're bringing* on their home screen (with **Mark done**, which the organizer sees as ✓ done), but they can't open the event. Their Events page explains that the inventory's events are run by the group, and has no *New event*. When an inventory joins a group, the owner's app labels its sheet with the group's name (a Drive app property) so these people's apps know. The label also tells a new phone to look for the group: if the person has it (the owner, say), the group and its events load; if not, they get the one-inventory view (looked up again at most hourly).
- **First run**: a new inventory opens **Where do you keep things?** Tap Home, Storage unit, Car… and rooms like Garage or Kitchen to create them in one go. It's also under Places → *Quick-add rooms and places*.

## What's in v1

- **Quick capture:** photo (resized on the phone), name, what it's for, location, optional category/tags/qty/warranty/expiry/notes. "Save & add another" keeps the same location for bulk logging.
- **Location hierarchy:** Place → Room → Container, with any depth. Recently used spots appear first.
- **Search by purpose:** matches name, "what it's for", tags, category, location and notes, with prefix and typo tolerance and a few synonyms ("fix a leak" finds plumber's tape). Every word must match.
- **Photos now, details later** (for storage units, basements, anywhere without signal):
  - **Quick capture** (a place's page, or on Add): the camera stays open; one photo per thing, each kept on the phone at once. The place at the top says where they go; tap it to change it for the next photos. **Done** opens Photos to review.
  - **Fill in later** (on Add, once there's a photo): keeps the photo and place and starts the form again. Offered in the message too when AI can't be reached (offline, or no answer within 20 seconds).
  - **Photos to review** (Find shows a card with the count; `#/review`): these are **only on this phone** (photos and previews in IndexedDB, the list in `paran.drafts.<inventory>`) until someone checks and saves them; then they're ordinary items that sync to Google for everyone. With AI on, they're **filled in automatically** whenever Paran is open and online (on by default; a switch on that screen turns it off), one at a time at about 13 a minute (under the free key's 15), retried up to three times, pausing for the day's allowance or a key problem. A web app can't run while closed, so nothing happens with Paran closed.
  - **Check one by one** opens each in the Add form, filled in with AI's suggestion (marked as suggested, as usual), or the several-things review for a photo with more than one thing; **Save & next**, **Skip** or **Delete photo**. **Save … as AI named them** saves, after one confirmation, the photos AI named as one thing that isn't like anything already there; the rest stay to check. **Select** → **Fill in with AI** (also with automatic off) or **Delete**. AI accuracy is recorded as for any photo. Start fresh warns that waiting photos will be deleted.
- **Moving things around (every move has Undo):**
  - *One item:* **Move** on the item screen.
  - *Several items:* tap **Select** above any list (or long-press a card), tick items, then **Move**.
  - *From the destination:* open a place and tap **Bring items here** to search and tick items from anywhere.
  - *A whole bin or shelf:* open it and tap **Move this spot**; everything inside goes with it.
  - The place picker has a search box ("box 3", "garage shelf") and can create a new spot on the spot.
  - *To another inventory of the group* (the school's storage → a volunteer's garage the school keeps as its own inventory): the picker for **Move** (item page, or Select → Move) lists **Other inventories in <group>**; tap one, then a place there (or *no place yet*), and confirm. The item goes with its id, photo, details, status (lent stays lent) and history; it's written to that inventory's sheet first, with its History rows and a *Moved to … from …* line, and only then removed here (with the same line in this inventory's History), so a refusal leaves it where it was. Group events that use it look for it there. Needs a connection and edit access to both inventories (checked with Drive first: "You can view Storage unit, but not change it"). No Undo; the toast offers **Open <inventory>**. The full photo stays in its original Photos folder (people with access only to the new inventory see the preview). For a few things at someone's home, lending them to that person is simpler.
- **Reserve:** **Lend or reserve** on an item has two modes. *Lend now* is one tap on a name. *Reserve* holds it for someone with an optional pick-up-by date (Today, Tomorrow, In 3 days, Next week, or any date). Reserved items show "Reserved · Ravi · pick up by Thu" (red once late), stay reserved if moved, and appear in the **Out** tab and the person's view. **Picked up** turns it into a lend; **Cancel** (with Undo) releases it. Lending something reserved for someone else asks first. The date is stored in a new `dueAt` column, added automatically to older sheets after `thumb`.
- **More photos per item:** up to 6 (the main photo + 5), from any angle: other sides, labels, the item next to something for size. Add several at once from the camera or library. Tap any photo to swipe through them full screen; extra photos can be removed there.
- **AI merges what it sees across all photos** (when AI is on): each time photos are added it looks at all of the item's photos together and fills in what's missing: what it's for, category, search words, **size**, and the **model/specs** read from labels (added to Notes). It never overwrites what you typed; on a saved item there's Undo.
- **Size:** optional one-tap size on every item (Pocket, Hand-held, Shoebox, Carry-on, Large, Furniture), filled in by AI when it can tell from the photos. Shown on the item page and searchable ("hand-held", "small", "large").
- **Photos open inside the app:** tap an item's photo for full screen. Full-size photos are cached on the phone (most recent 150), so they open instantly and offline. Photos another member added show the preview, with a link to their Drive for the original.
- **Item history:** each item's page ends with a **History** list, newest first (last 5, then *Show all*): added, moved (from → to), edited (what changed, e.g. "Name: Stud finder → Zircon stud finder · Qty: 1 → 2"), saved for (with pick-up-by date), picked up, lent, returned, missing/found, with date, time and who did it when it wasn't you.
- **Before / after in History, with Restore:** whenever details change, by a person or by AI from photos, the item's History shows each changed field as old (struck through) → new. **Restore** (or **Restore all**) opens Edit with the earlier text filled in and highlighted; tap **Save** to restore it, or Cancel to leave things as they are. Exact old/new values are kept in the Log's `changes` column.
- **AI refines only its own work:** on a saved item, new photos can update fields AI filled earlier (recorded when the item was added), and fill empty ones, but never change text someone typed or restored.
- **People you lend to:** "Lend" takes a free-text name and drops down matching people you've lent to before (typing "RAVI" reuses "Ravi", so one person stays one person). Tap a borrower's name (on an item, in the **Lent out** filter's "Who has your things" strip, or in **More → People you lend to**) to see what they have now with *Got it back* / *Got everything back*, and their full history of items borrowed and returned with dates. People are derived from the **Log** tab, so there's no separate list to maintain.
- **Duplicate check (on the phone, no AI):** saving a new item whose name matches an existing one (plurals, punctuation, small typos and word order ignored) asks first: *Open it*, *Same one, it's here now* (moves it), *I have another (+qty)*, or *Save as a new item*. Matches in the same place are flagged more readily. In the AI multi-item review, items already in the chosen place are labelled "Already here" and start unticked. Save locks after the first tap.
- **Status:** Move, Lend (who has it), Got it back, Can't find / Found it. Every change is appended to the **Log** tab.
- **QR labels:** print a label for any bin or shelf. Scanning it with the phone camera opens that place in the app.
- **Sharing with people:** the owner invites by email (edit access to the sheet, view access to photos). They sign in and picks "Join a shared inventory".
- **Offline:** the app shell is cached and data is cached on the device. Changes made offline queue up and sync when the connection returns.
- **Export:** CSV (opens in Excel), or just open the Google Sheet.
- **Scan a QR label in the app:** the QR button in the Find search bar (and **Scan label** on Places) opens the camera inside Paran, so iPhone doesn't hand the link to Safari. Scanning a box or shelf label offers **See what's inside**, **Put items away here** (pick existing items to move in), **Add a new item here** and **Scan many into it**. Any place picker (Move, bulk Move, Add form) also has a QR button: scan the label on the box and the item goes there. Labels from another inventory or non-Paran codes are rejected with a message. Decoding uses the browser's BarcodeDetector where available (Android Chrome), otherwise the bundled jsQR (Apache-2.0, `js/vendor`).
- **Share with anyone (a copy, no app needed):** the share button on an item sends its photo, what it's for and (optionally) where it's kept through the phone's share sheet (WhatsApp, Messages, email). On a place, the share button (or ⋯ → *Share what's here*) sends everything inside, grouped by spot, as a **List** (text) or a **Picture** with photos (up to 80 items), optionally with what each is for. In **Select** mode, **Share** (next to Move) sends just the chosen items, as one list or grouped by where they're kept. Lists (Select mode and places) can go out as **List** (text), **Picture** (one image with thumbnails) or **Photos** (each item's full main photo as separate images, up to 10, with the list as the message). Recipients get a one-time copy; nothing in the inventory is shared. Without a share sheet (desktop), the text is copied and the picture downloaded.
- **Scan many (bulk onboarding, AI):** on any place, tap **Scan many**, then pan the camera slowly across a shelf or room. Auto mode keeps the sharpest new view every 2 s (up to 8 photos); you can also tap the shutter, remove a photo from the strip, or choose photos if the camera isn't available. All photos go to Gemini in **one request**, which returns each physical item once with the photo that shows it best; Stash crops each item and opens the review list (untick, rename, *Put somewhere inside* per item, Tick all). A one-time note explains that wide shots show more of the home. History records these items as added by *AI scan*.
- **AI suggestions (optional, off by default):** each user turns it on in **More → AI suggestions** ("Name things from a photo"). The sheet is built to be done, not read: an example of the result, then three steps with one button each (**Open Google AI Studio** → *Create API key, Copy* → **Paste**), with the current step highlighted and coming back from AI Studio jumping to Paste. **Turn on** stays off until something that could be a key is pasted (20+ characters, no spaces; key formats vary, older ones start `AIza`), and the key is checked with Google before saving. The privacy notice is one line by the button (photos go straight to Google; free keys may be reviewed, so skip IDs and private papers) with **More** for the full notice; turning on is the agreement. The key is stored only on that device, never in the Sheet. A per-photo switch on the Add screen ("Suggest details from photos") controls whether a photo is analyzed.
  - **Event from words:** on New event, *What does it need?* takes a description or a pasted list (WhatsApp, email, notes); on an event, **From a list** does the same. Gemini gets only the text and the names (and counts) of what the event can draw on, never photos, and returns sections, lines and counts (estimates flagged "≈") plus which existing items fit. A review sheet lets you untick, rename, change counts and pick the item for each line (or *Not in inventory yet*); names the AI invents are ignored. Event name, date and notes are filled in only where you left them empty.
  - **Receipts:** **Scan a receipt** on Add (or **Bought some? Scan the receipt** on an event with things still to buy) takes a photo or a screenshot of an online order. Each product comes back with a plain name, qty, price, brand/model, category, purpose, search words and any printed warranty. Things you already have are offered as *Add N to your …* or *just add the receipt* (each place listed when the same thing is kept in two); things used up start unticked unless they're for an event; anything matching an event line still to buy is linked to it, so the line turns ready. New items go in the place you pick. Saved per item: `price` ("24.99 USD"), `store`, `boughtAt`, `warranty` (bought date + months), brand/model and return-by date in notes, and `receipt`: one photo of the receipt shared by all its items (uploaded to Drive like photos; **Receipt** on the item opens it). Card digits, names and addresses aren't asked for or kept. **Total check:** the products' prices are added up and compared with the printed subtotal (or the total minus tax and fees). Within 1% shows "✓ Adds up to the receipt's subtotal"; otherwise an amber note gives both amounts and the gap ("$24.99 short … a line may be missing or a price misread"), so a bad read is caught before saving. Price, store and date can also be typed under *More details*.
  - **How accurate it's been** (More → AI suggestions): every use of AI (a photo, a scan, a receipt, an event list) adds one row to the inventory's History (Log tab, action `ai`) with how many suggestions it offered, how many were kept, and per field how close the suggestion was to what was saved, plus whether a receipt added up. **Weighted, not right/wrong:** text scores the average of letter-level closeness and shared words (a typo fix or a plural still counts as the same word), so "Folding table, 6 ft" saved as "Folding table, 6 ft, Lifetime" is ~77% right and "Stud finder" → "Stud sensor" ~57%; numbers score by closeness (9 for 10 = 90%); picks from a list (which item, which event line, category) are right or wrong. The card shows "% right" plus how many were saved exactly as suggested, and "AI names, weeks later" scores later renames the same way. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  - One item in the photo: empty fields are filled and marked "AI suggested" until edited. Anything the user typed is never overwritten.
  - Several items (an open bin or shelf): a review sheet lists each with a cropped photo. Untick, rename, choose the location, then **Add selected**.
  - Model `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` (falls back to `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`). Calls are paced under 15 per minute; hitting the daily limit shows a clear message and the user carries on manually.
  - Free-tier note shown at opt-in: Google may use free-tier content to improve its products, so don't analyze IDs or private documents.
- **Demo mode:** try everything without Google; data stays in that browser.

cd stash

It has to be served over http(s), not opened as a file, because it uses ES modules and a service worker.

## Google Cloud setup (one time, about 10 minutes)

1. Go to <https://console.cloud.google.com/>, create a project (e.g. `stash-inventory`).
2. **APIs & Services → Library:** enable **Google Drive API**, **Google Sheets API**, and **Google Picker API**.
3. **APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen** (Google Auth Platform → Branding / Audience / Data access):
   - User type **External**, app name "Stash", your email as support contact.
   - Data access → add scope `.../auth/drive.file` (plus `openid`, `email`, `profile`).
   - Audience → while in **Testing**, add each person's Gmail under **Test users** (up to 100). Users not on the list can't sign in until you publish the app.
4. **Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID** → type **Web application**.
   - Authorized JavaScript origins: `http://localhost:8080` and your hosting origin, e.g. `https://aarvakuzhu.github.io`. Origins only, with no path and no trailing slash.
   - No redirect URIs are needed (the app uses the popup token flow).
   - Copy the **Client ID** into `CLIENT_ID` in `js/config.js`.
5. **Credentials → Create credentials → API key** → restrict it to **Google Picker API** and to your website origins (HTTP referrers). Put it in `API_KEY`.
6. **Project number** (Dashboard → Project info, digits only) → `APP_ID`. The Picker needs it so a joined sheet is granted to the app.

The Client ID and API key are not secrets in a browser app; they are protected by the origin restrictions above.

## Host on GitHub Pages

1. Create a repo (e.g. `aarvakuzhu/paran`) and push the contents of this folder to `main`.
2. Repo **Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → main / (root)**.
3. The app is at `https://aarvakuzhu.github.io/paran/`. Make sure `https://aarvakuzhu.github.io` is in the OAuth client's JavaScript origins.
4. On the phone, open the URL, then **Share → Add to Home Screen** (iOS) or **Install app** (Android Chrome).

After changing files, bump `VERSION` in `sw.js`. Stash opens from the copy on the phone (no network wait). A new version downloads in the background as one complete set (each file requested as `file?v=VERSION`, so GitHub's CDN can't hand back a stale copy mid-deploy) and is applied straight away if the app was just opened, or via the **Reload** prompt. If the app ever fails to start (e.g. a broken update), a startup check works out why within seconds (a new version still downloading, offline, a file that didn't load, an error, an older version on the phone than on the server, or just a slow connection) and says that, with versions shown, plus the matching button: **Get the latest version** re-downloads the app files and never touches the inventory.

## How sharing works (and its limits)

- The owner taps **More → Invite**. That shares the sheet (edit) and the root folder (view) with the email and adds a row to **Members**.
- The invited person opens Paran, signs in, and chooses **Join a shared inventory**. The Google Picker grants the app access to that one sheet.
- Each item stores a small thumbnail (≈20 KB) inside the sheet, so **everyone always sees every photo thumbnail**. The full-size photo opens in Drive.
- Photos a member adds go to a "Photos – <inventory>" folder inside *their* Paran folder. It is shared with everyone on the Members list at the time they join; people invited later see the thumbnail, and need the photo shared to open the full size.

## Data model (Google Sheet)

| Tab | Columns |
|---|---|
| Items | id, name, purpose, category, tags, locationId, qty, status (home/lent/reserved/missing), lentTo (person), photoId, photoUrl, warranty, expiry, notes, addedBy, addedAt, updatedAt, updatedBy, deleted, thumb, dueAt (pick-up-by date), size, photos (additional photos: Drive id, link, small preview) |
| Locations | id, name, parentId, kind (place/room/container), notes, createdBy, createdAt, deleted |
| Members | email, role, photoFolderId, invitedBy, joinedAt |
| Log | ts, itemId, action, detail, by, info (what changed / moved from / pick-up date), changes (exact before/after per field, or which fields AI suggested when added) |
| Events | id, name, date (YYYY-MM-DD, optional), notes, createdBy, createdAt, updatedAt, updatedBy, deleted |
| EventLines | id, eventId, section, itemId (empty = not in an inventory yet), name (the item's name when added, or the needed thing's name), qty (how many needed), note, sort, updatedAt, updatedBy, deleted, invId (which inventory the item is in; set on group events), sources (JSON list of every item the line draws on: inventory, item id, name, and `off` when marked not for this event; itemId/invId mirror the first), bringers (JSON list of who's bringing it: name, qty, email of their Google account if linked; qty 0 on older entries = the rest), got (JSON list of what arrived on the day: who, n), after (JSON: state back/moved/missing/damaged/kept/gone, note, to, at, by) |
| Tasks | id, eventId, eventName, eventDate, lineId, email, name, qty, todo, section, done, updatedAt, updatedBy, deleted — in an inventory sheet, one row per line given to someone with access to that inventory only. The organizer's app writes all but `done`; the assignee's app writes only `done`. Removing them from the line marks the row deleted. |

An inventory's own events are in its sheet; a group's events are in the same two tabs of the group's **directory** sheet. The tabs are added automatically the first time an event is saved there (and to older inventory sheets when someone who can edit opens the app).

Rules for editing the sheet by hand: it's fine to edit cell values and to sort or filter **in a filter view**. Don't delete rows or rename the header row, because the app matches rows by `id` and columns by header name. Removing an item in the app sets `deleted`; it never deletes rows.

## Known limits in v1

- **Sign-in:** a browser-only app can't hold a long-lived Google session (that needs a server). Stash keeps Google's one-hour pass across app restarts, always opens straight into your inventory from the phone's copy, and renews the pass on your first tap after it runs out, using your saved account, so you normally see at most a brief Google popup and no account picker. Edits made meanwhile wait in the queue; the pill shows **Tap to sync**.
- If two people edit the *same* item at the same moment, the last save wins.
- AI suggestions use each user's own Gemini quota; the "photos analyzed today" count is per device, while Google's limit is per key's project.
- Google accounts only.
